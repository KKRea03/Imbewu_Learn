WHY IMBEWU LEARN

Knowledge shouldn't have to be lost to be learned from.

Educators and knowledge keepers hold generations of lived experience — in medicine, agriculture, craft, story, and custom. Imbewu Learn gives that knowledge a verified, respectful path to learners everywhere.

Every submission is reviewed by a trusted Knowledge Keeper before anything is published, and only verified knowledge is ever turned into a lesson.

HOW IT WORKS

From a spoken memory to a structured lesson.

01

Share

An educator records their knowledge directly in the browser, or uploads a recording, and submits it for review.

02

Verify

A Knowledge Keeper listens, reviews the transcription and metadata, and approves or rejects the submission.

03

Learn

Approved knowledge is turned into a lesson, summary, quiz, and translation — ready for learners to explore.
